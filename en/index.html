The thesis

An asset paid at the source, and short of capital

Paid straight
from payroll

The employer or payroll office deducts the installment before paying the salary or pension. The borrower doesn’t decide whether to pay this month.

Stable, granular
cash flows

How it works

From capital to portfolio, and back

01

Funding

The investor lends to Finmobil through a private debt instrument with a set rate, term and payment schedule.

Structure and protections

Built to protect capital before it earns

Asset segregation, originator alignment and visibility into the underlying asset.

Segregated trust

The portfolio and its collections sit in a fiduciary vehicle (patrimonio autónomo), separate from Finmobil’s and the originator’s balance sheets.

Aligned originator

A holdback on part of the purchase price and an obligation to repurchase loans that fail the criteria.

Eligibility criteria

Limits per borrower, payroll payer and originator; loan rate, term and profile set before each purchase.

Full traceability

Tokenized registry of the portfolio, with loan-level reporting on collections, delinquencies and prepayments.

Risks

We know them, and we manage them

Credit

Borrower job loss or death. Mitigated with insurance, diversification and concentration limits.

Payroll payer

Employer delays in remitting collections. Mitigated with per-payer limits and monthly monitoring.

Originator

Servicing failures or weak portfolio quality. Mitigated with due diligence, holdbacks and a backup servicer.

Regulatory and FX

Regulatory or exchange-rate changes in Colombia and Mexico. Detailed in the investment memorandum.

About

Who we are
