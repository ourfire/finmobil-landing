The thesis

An asset paid at the source that is short of capital

Paid straight
from payroll

The payroll payer deducts the installment before paying the wage or pension: the borrower doesn’t decide whether to pay this month. In Colombia this is called libranza .

Stable, granular
cash flows

Thousands of small loans spread across many payroll payers, with low correlation to public markets.

Originators
short of funding

Credit unions and originators have loan demand they can’t meet for lack of liquidity. That’s where we come in: we buy their portfolios.

How it works

From capital to loans, and back

01

Funding

The investor lends to Finmobil through a private debt instrument with a set rate, term and payment schedule.

Protections

How capital is protected

Segregated trust

The portfolio and its collections sit in a trust managed by Fiduciaria Bancolombia, separate from the balance sheets of Finmobil and the originator.

Holdback and repurchase

The originator leaves part of the price as security and buys back loans that fail the criteria. It shares the risk with us.

Eligibility criteria

Before each purchase we set rate, term, borrower profile, and limits by borrower, payroll payer and originator.

Traceability

A loan-by-loan digital record of the portfolio, with periodic reports on collections, delinquencies and prepayments.

Risks

Credit

RiskBorrower job loss or death.

MitigantInsurance, diversification and concentration limits.

Payroll payer

RiskEmployer delays in remitting deductions.

MitigantLimits per payroll payer and monthly monitoring of collections.

Originator

RiskServicing failures or weak portfolio quality.

MitigantDue diligence, holdback and repurchase, and a backup servicer .

Regulatory and FX

RiskRegulatory or exchange-rate changes in Colombia and Mexico.

MitigantAssessed per transaction and detailed in the investment memorandum .
